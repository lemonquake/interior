Downloaded reference (not used directly): "AAA Fake Volumetric Clouds" by Velvet Katana Studios,
https://godotshaders.com/shader/aaa-fake-volumetric-clouds/ - CC0 ("The shader code and all code snippets in this
post are under CC0 license and can be used freely without the author's permission").

scripts/night_sky.gdshader (0.8) adopts its approach for the moonlit clouds: a cloud deck projected onto a plane
(dir.xz / (dir.y + k)), three texture-noise layers (base, larger secondary, subtracted detail) plus an edge-turbulence
term, a soft coverage threshold, thickness-based body shadowing with bright thin edges lit by the light (here the
moon), occlusion of the light's disc and glow by thick cloud, and blending toward the sky colour near the horizon.
The noise texture is generated by tools/assets/make_cloud_noise.py (assets/fields/sky/cloud_noise.png).
