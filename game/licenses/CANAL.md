# Canal settlement — 0.17 (abandoned squatter settlement on a river ravine)

The owner's photograph of a Philippine riverside settlement is a layout and atmosphere reference only; none of its pixels are used.

All new 0.17 models were made for this project and carry no third-party rights:

- 32 stilt shanties and 15 rubbish/rubble pieces, generated in Blender 5.2 by `tools/blender/canal/build_canal_kit.py` (output in `game/assets/models/canal/kit/`, with `kit.json`).
- 38 vandalism decals (random spray symbols, scribbled tags, bubble letters, relocation-census house numbers, brushed warning signs), generated by `tools/assets/make_canal_graffiti.py` into `game/assets/outdoor/canal/tags/`. Tag letters are random; no real crew names, logos or artwork.
- Shaders `canal_bank.gdshader` (ravine ground and rubble masonry), `canal_river.gdshader` (river; reuses the project's night-sky include) and `canal_paint.gdshader` (faded house paint).

Textures reused from the project's existing CC0 Poly Haven sets (credited in ASSET_LICENSES.md): dark wooden planks, blue painted planks, rusty metal 04, rusty corrugated iron, corrugated iron 02, concrete block wall, rough plaster broken, concrete wall 008, peeling painted wall, painted metal shutter, brown mud 02, muddy tracks, withered grass, leafy grass, fabric pattern 07. Reused models (credited in THIRD_PARTY_MODELS.md / below): trees, banana plants, bamboo, shrub, coconut palm, ferns, reeds, cattails, fallen trunk, root cluster, dead trunk, rocks, oil drum, monobloc chair, tricycle, Poly Haven trashbag and wooden crate.

# Canal settlement — 0.16

The attached photograph is a layout and atmosphere reference. Its pixels and watermark are not used as game textures.

New downloaded assets, retrieved 2026-10-08, under CC0 1.0:

- [Trashbag](https://polyhaven.com/a/trashbag), Benny Weimer / Poly Haven. Original 1K glTF with normal, albedo and packed material maps.
- [Wooden Crate 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_crate_01), Poly Haven. Original 1K glTF with normal, albedo and packed material maps.

Each model's `source.json` records exact download URLs and SHA-256 checksums. The importer checks upstream MD5 values before accepting files. [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Existing credited assets reused: Poly Haven dirt, mud, peeling plaster, rust, corrugated iron, timber, fabric and leaf textures; the project's oil drum, plastic chair, pail, ferns, reeds, bananas and trees. Their original credits remain in ASSET_LICENSES.md and THIRD_PARTY_MODELS.md. Water reuses the credited field-water shader.

The metal footstep variations mix Kenney's CC0 concrete footfalls with damped metal impact recordings. Dirt variations filter the existing CC0 grass footfalls. Both sets have five variants, generated by `tools/assets/make_canal_audio.py`. Original wood footsteps are retained.

The settlement architecture, supports, roofs, nail details, railings, stairs, water platform, vines, graffiti, decals, light behavior and layout were authored locally. Graffiti sources are in `tools/assets/make_canal_graffiti.py`; these contain no downloaded artwork.
