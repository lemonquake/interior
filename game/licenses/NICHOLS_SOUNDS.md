# Nichols house sounds (0.21)

Door recordings from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded and processed by `tools/assets/fetch_nichols.py` (mono, high-passed at
60 Hz, silence trimmed, normalised) into `game/assets/audio/nichols/`, played by `game/scripts/nichols_door.gd` and
`nichols_locked.gd`. Each adaptation is shared under the original's licence (CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
