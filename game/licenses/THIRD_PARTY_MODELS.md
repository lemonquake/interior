### Moon and water

- Moon colour map: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (LRO colour mosaic), public domain,
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (`game/assets/fields/sky/moon_color.jpg`).
- Water: "Water Shader" by StayAtHomeDev (uploaded by Verssales), CC0, https://godotshaders.com/shader/water-shader/;
  the downloaded original is `game/assets/fields/water/water_stayathomedev_cc0.gdshader`, and
  `game/scripts/night_water.gdshader` is built on it.
- The SIRADO sign, rice, grass, dikes, road, pond, fireflies and night sky are generated in this project.


## 0.8: mist and clouds

- Ground mist: "Moving gradient noise fog/ mist for Godot 4" by alghost, MIT,
  https://godotshaders.com/shader/moving-gradient-noise-fog-mist-for-godot-4/ (gradient noise by Inigo Quilez, MIT).
  The downloaded original with its licence is `game/assets/fields/fog/mist_alghost_mit.gdshader.txt`;
  `game/scripts/night_mist.gdshader` is adapted from it.
- Clouds: "AAA Fake Volumetric Clouds" by Velvet Katana Studios, CC0,
  https://godotshaders.com/shader/aaa-fake-volumetric-clouds/; its technique (see
  `game/assets/fields/sky/clouds_velvetkatana_cc0.txt`) drives the clouds in `game/scripts/night_sky.gdshader`. The
  cloud noise texture is generated by `tools/assets/make_cloud_noise.py`.


## 0.10: the hut, the drawer and the Glock

- Glock 17 (`game/assets/models/story/glock17.glb`): "[ FREE ] Rigged Semi-Auto Pistol G17" by nixo_design,
  CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/0c7e4441d9a1432f9abb61911c95a8a2 (via the Objaverse mirror). Kept as
  separate animated parts with its firing animation (trigger, slide, ejected round); the heavy grip and slide shells
  were decimated and the gun scaled to 186 mm by `tools/blender/process_glock.py`.
- The hut's interior lining, dresser, drawer, candle, key, footpath and ammo box are generated in
  `game/scripts/hut_house.gd`, using the project's existing walnut, oak and bamboo textures.
