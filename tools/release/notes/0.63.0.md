- New chapter after Heavy-arm: Outer Street. Ten objectives from PILCST's rear gate to the bridge over the river.

- The subway shutter is chained: find bolt cutters in the hardware store up the yellow stairs (or round by the covered footbridge). Something is shut in its back room.
- A dead subway station: find the power room, throw the main breaker, and the lights flicker back on. So does everything down on the platform.
- A stalled train fills the tunnel: go through it car to car, then follow the tunnel to the emergency exit and the long stair up.
- The river road to the bridge, with everything the station woke behind you.
- PILCST: after the gas-store blast the way down is clear: the desks across the second-floor corridor are thrown down, and a rubble ramp runs from the lean-to roof into the field.
- PILCST: the door at the top of the maintenance ladder can be forced open (loud). The duct is still the quiet way.
