# 0.64.0
- Android: the squad game is on phones. Phones and PCs play in the same squads (both on 0.64.0), or a phone hosts with the AI squad.
- Android: all the gore is on phones now: blood splats, drips, pools, wounds on the bodies, torn-off limbs and gibs (they were switched off there).
- Android: no more getting stuck on the loading screen. A phone reads only what it builds and keeps far less in memory.
- Android: new touch controls laid out like a phone shooter: a floating stick (all the way forward sprints), drag to look, a big FIRE you can aim with while it fires (hold for automatic), a second FIRE on the left, ADS, RELOAD, JUMP, CROUCH, SHOVE and GRENADE, USE when there is something to use, and the weapon row at the top.
- Android: the squad game starts on Easy. The picture fills wide screens, and the 3D resolution steps down a little when a phone falls behind.
- Squad game: the crouch hole to the rice fields and the secret crawl to the Nichols property are nailed shut. Phones let go of both places for the squad game.
