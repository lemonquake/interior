- Outer Street: past the long stair, a row of buildings you go through: Airah's Pizzahan, a court, the flats, a red-stair alley with an eatery upstairs, Nichols Davao Mart (two floors), Azrin & Azrael (two floors) and a funeral parlor.
- Nichols Davao Mart: the way up is the broken escalator. Drag a table under its end, climb onto it and jump for it.
- The bridge is much longer: wrecked cars, vans, taxis, buses and a jack-knifed truck across it, and a stretch where half the deck has fallen into the river.
- New bus and train models: the doors are torn off or forced and the windows broken, so the way through can be seen.
- Faster on phones and PCs: far fewer things drawn separately in the squad game's districts, fewer stutters in big fights, and phones read the town's infected while a menu is open instead of in the middle of a fight. Physics, bodies and gore are unchanged.
