# 0.66.0
- Play now goes straight to the squad game. The solo story is switched off for now.
- Dodong waits outside your gate at the start, flashlight in hand, and leads the squad all the way to his house: down the lane, over the canal bridge, along both banks and into the warren.
- The Old Salmonan gate rolls shut behind the squad on Store Road. Everything back there is let go of, so the rest of the night runs lighter.
- Smoother fights: a school floor's infected, a class, the horde past room 501, Heavy-arm's pack and Outer Street's crowds now arrive over a few frames instead of all in one long stutter.
- Fewer separate draws along the canal, and the rice fields and the Nichols property are no longer loaded in the squad game on PCs either. Physics, bodies and gore are unchanged.
