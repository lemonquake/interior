# 0.67.0
- Options from the pause menu: graphics, sound and display can be changed mid-game. The squad game keeps running for everyone else while you are in it.
- Outer Street is darker and fuller: a rescue tent on the plaza that the night overran, the dead along the avenue, the overpass, the subway and the tunnel, the train's lights dying as you reach its second car, and the long stair going dark with something coming up behind you.
- The kiosk's door at the top of the long stair slams shut behind the squad, and everything back to the avenue is let go of, so Market Row and the bridge run lighter.
- Fixed two gaps in the floor (the subway's power room doorway and the kiosk's door) that let dropped things fall out of the world.
