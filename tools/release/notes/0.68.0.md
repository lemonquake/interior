# 0.68.0
- A new chapter after the long bridge: Davao Central Laboratory. Once everyone crosses the bridge, the board loads the hospital and the squad starts outside it. Ambulances, police cars and the doctors, nurses and officers who stayed outside are by the door, and every street is blocked.
- Inside: the lobby's stairs have collapsed and the corridors are barricaded or caved in. Find the way up through the ER and the back stairwell. Each floor locks behind you (the lockdown shutters, the fire door) and is unloaded.
- Doctor Berido is hiding somewhere on the second floor. He shows the way up: the cafeteria kitchen's ladder into the vents, a crawl to the records room, and the stair hidden behind its bookcase.
- The third floor is quiet. Find the five things Doctor Berido needs (the keycard, the logbook, the override key, the fuse, the battery pack) to open the laboratory's electric door.
- The secret laboratory has brain tanks, pods, a yellow-railed catwalk and its staff, who have all turned. Then Doctor Lucky, the Morboid: fast, with big bone claws and a jaw that hangs open. He leaps across the hall, climbs the walls and drops off them onto the squad. His health shows as a heart monitor's trace.
- 12 new infected for the hospital: surgeons, scientists, a hazmat suit, a cleanroom suit, a paramedic, a guard, a patient, a burn-ward patient, a head nurse and a morgue attendant.
- The run now ends on the hospital's roof with a flare.
