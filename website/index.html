Co-op

Nobody gets through this night alone

Squads of up to five fight the same infected, in the same rooms, with the same doors and tables. The host's game runs the night; the Sleep server carries everyone's connection to it, so a friend across the city or across the country joins with one click.

Host in one click

Press Host game. Your squad is listed for everyone online, and friends join with Join. The connection goes through the Sleep server, so it works behind ordinary home routers and on mobile data. On the same Wi-Fi it connects you directly.

A squad that talks

Squad AI fill any empty place and call out what they see: contacts, reloads, out of ammo, “behind you”. You have a voice menu of your own, a squad chat and a lobby chat, and a ready check before every chapter.

Down, not out

A teammate at 60 HP or less gets a red, beating name tag. A downed survivor crawls and gets a DOWN marker with their name and distance, seen through walls. Get to them with a healthkit and help them up.

Pick the night you want

Easy: rifle, Glock, a grenade and a kit each. Normal: a Glock and one kit. Hard: a Glock with fifty rounds and nothing else. Every live grenade is lit and marked on screen with its fuse burning down.

The infected

Everyone here used to be someone

Every infected is a person from the town: a firefighter still in his helmet, a teacher, a nun, a jogger, a grandmother. Thirteen kinds behave differently. And they come apart the way bodies do: hands, forearms, feet and shins first, then the trunk. A shotgun fires nine real buckshot balls; what you shoot off stays where it fell.

One body, built many ways: every infected shares the same rig, then gets its own outfit, hair, skin, wounds and missing parts. That's why no two in a crowd look alike, and why each piece can come off on its own.

The guard

Heavy-arm

Mang Dado worked the night shift as a security guard. He still carries everything a security guard carries: the old flashlight on his lanyard hunts for whoever he wants and lies long down the ground where he is about to charge. Rounds barely hurt him. Blasts do. Bait the charge into the fuel drums.

The night

A town you can walk through, and wish you hadn't

Seen through a body camera: a wide lens with dark corners, grain that rises in the dark, a smear on fast turns. The town is lit only by the moon, its own lamps and your flashlight. Every street, yard and room is walkable, and most of what's in them can be pushed, dragged or knocked over.

Story, alone

The solo story (home, the three notes, the man in the black hoodie, the rice fields) is switched off for now: Play goes straight to the squad game.

Chapters, together

Dodong waits outside your gate. Escort him home, take the keys, hold Olivar Family Mart, find Jonna, cut through the backstreets to the college, get out past Heavy-arm, then on through Outer Street, the subway and Market Row to the long bridge. Each gate and door shuts behind you; a loading board waits for the whole squad to be ready.

Physical everything

Doors and gates swing on real hinges, chairs slide when you walk into them, tables can be dragged into doorways and a grenade throws them across the room. Shots go through the open frames.

Playing with friends

Everyone needs the same version: the launcher sees to that. One of you presses Play → Host game; everyone else presses Play and then Join on that game. The host picks the mode and starts the mission when the squad is ready. Up to five play; empty places can be filled with squad AI. Esc (Pause on a phone) brings up the pause, and Options there changes the graphics, sound and display while the squad plays on.
